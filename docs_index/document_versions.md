## Latest Manuscript Theme

The latest revised manuscript is titled:

`Simulation of Subauroral Polarization Streams Effects on the Nightside Airglow Emissions`

The key mechanism statement is that SAPS-induced neutral upwelling increases molecular O2 and N2 densities, promoting charge transfer and O2+ density enhancement, which leads to SAR-arc-like 630.0 nm emission through dissociative recombination.
